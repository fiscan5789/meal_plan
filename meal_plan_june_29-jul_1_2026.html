Loaded Beef & Black Bean
Skillet with Red Pepper

Generated recipe — adapts the structure of the Cheesy, Spicy Black Bean Bake from Easy Weeknight Dinners (Ali Slagle) with browned ground beef and red pepper added. Not a direct extraction.

25 minactive
Oven 475°Foven-safe skillet
4 servings+ toddler portions

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbspextra-virgin olive oil (pantry)
  • 1 lbground beef (90% lean) (your beef)
  • 1red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, diced (your red pepper)
  • 1yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 5 clovesgarlic, sliced
  • ¼ cuptomato paste
  • 1½ tspsmoked paprika
  • 1 tspground cumin
  • ¼ tspred pepper flakes (pantry)
  • 2 (14-oz) cansblack beans, drained & rinsed (your black beans)
  • ½ cupboiling water
  • 1½ cupsgrated sharp Cheddar (6 oz)
  • —kosher salt & black pepper (pantry)
  • to servewarm tortillas, rice, or a green salad; cilantro

Method

Heat the oven to 475°F. In a 10–12" oven-safe skillet, heat the olive oil over medium-high. Add the ground beef and a pinch of salt; cook, breaking it up, until browned and fully cooked through with no pink, 6–7 min. Pregnancy: cook to 160°F. Use a slotted spoon to move the beef to a bowl, leaving the fat in the skillet.
Add the diced red pepper and onion to the skillet. Cook, stirring, until softened and starting to brown, about 5 min. Add the sliced garlic and cook 1 min more.
Stir in the tomato paste, smoked paprika, cumin, and red pepper flakes. Fry 30 seconds, lowering the heat as needed so the garlic and paste don't burn.
Return the beef to the skillet. Add the black beans, boiling water, and generous pinches of salt and pepper. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
Sprinkle the cheese evenly over the top. Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake until the cheese melts and bubbles, 5–10 min. For more browning, run under the broiler 1–2 min.
Scatter with cilantro and serve with tortillas, rice, or a green salad.
Pregnancy Brown the beef fully (160°F, no pink) before it goes back in for the bake. Iron and folate together here — beef plus black beans — with the tomato paste and red pepper providing vitamin C to boost iron absorption. The cheese is cooked. A genuinely nutritious, easy first-trimester meal.
Make ahead (tomorrow once groceries arrive, ~5 min) Dice the red pepper and onion; slice the garlic; grate the cheese. Drain and rinse the beans. Everything else is a fast same-night cook. Nothing needs doing tonight.
For the 13-mo & 34-mo Black beans are a known kid favorite. This has red pepper flakes — scoop a small portion for the 13-mo before the spices go in (or use a light hand), mash the beans slightly, and cut the beef fine. For the 34-mo, serve with a tortilla to scoop; the melted cheese is a reliable win. Both kids get a good iron hit from this one.

Salmon Chraimeh

From Milk Street: Tuesday Nights by Christopher Kimball (2018). Salmon braised in a spiced tomato sauce.
20 mintotal
20 minactive
Stovetop10" skillet
4 servings+ toddler portions

Ingredients

  • 4 (6 oz)skin-on center-cut salmon fillets, 1–1½" thick (your 24 oz salmon)
  • 1 Tbspolive oil, plus more to serve (pantry)
  • 4scallions, thinly sliced (dark green parts reserved)
  • 3 clovesgarlic, thinly sliced
  • ½red bell pepper, seeded & thinly sliced (uses the other half of Monday's pepper, OR a fresh one)
  • 1 tspcoriander seeds (or ¾ tsp ground)
  • 1 tspcumin seeds (or ¾ tsp ground)
  • ¾ tspsmoked paprika
  • 14½-oz candiced tomatoes
  • 2 Tbspfinely chopped fresh mint
  • ¼ cupfresh cilantro, chopped
  • —kosher salt & black pepper (pantry)
  • to servelemon wedges; rice or crusty bread

Note: the original uses a sliced jalapeño for heat. I've swapped in red bell pepper for a mild, pregnancy-friendly version — add a pinch of red pepper flakes if you want some warmth.

Method

Season the salmon on both sides with salt and pepper.
In a 10" skillet over medium-high, heat the oil until shimmering. Add the white and light-green scallion parts, garlic, and sliced red pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned, about 2 min.
Stir in the coriander, cumin, and smoked paprika; cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
Stir in the diced tomatoes (with juices), ½ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper. Bring to a simmer, then nestle the fillets skin-side up in the sauce. Reduce to medium, cover, and simmer 6–8 min.
Pregnancy: cook the salmon fully through — to 145°F and opaque, not the medium the original targets. After the 6–8 min simmer, if it's not fully cooked, take the pan off heat and leave the fillets in the covered pan a few minutes more until done (the recipe's own note suggests exactly this for well-done fish).
Using tongs, peel off and discard the skin; transfer the fillets flesh-side up to plates. If the sauce is watery, simmer it 1–2 min to thicken. Off heat, stir in the mint and cilantro; taste and season.
Spoon the sauce over the salmon, sprinkle with the reserved scallion greens, drizzle with olive oil, and serve with lemon wedges and rice or bread.
Pregnancy Salmon is a top pregnancy fish — low-mercury, high in DHA. The only change from the book: cook it fully to 145°F (opaque, flakes easily) rather than the 115–120°F medium it calls for. The recipe conveniently tells you how to get there (rest covered off-heat). No alcohol; all spices are fine.
Make ahead (Monday evening, ~5 min) Slice the scallions (keep greens separate), garlic, and red pepper. Chop the mint and cilantro; store covered. Tuesday is then a 20-min cook start to finish — one of the fastest nights you'll have.
For the 13-mo & 34-mo This mild version (no jalapeño) is gentle enough for both kids. Flake a portion of cooked salmon, checking carefully for pin bones. The tomato sauce is fine — spoon a little over their fish or rice. For the 13-mo, skip the lemon (acidic) and go easy on the herbs. Salmon's omega-3s are great for them too.

Pasta alla Norma

From Milk Street: Tuesday Nights by Christopher Kimball (2018). Roasted eggplant & tomato pasta.
45 min total(eggplant roasts 30 unattended)
20 minactive
Oven 475°F + stovetop
4 servings+ toddler portions

Ingredients

  • 1 lbeggplant, peeled & cut into ¾" cubes
  • 6 Tbspextra-virgin olive oil, divided (pantry)
  • 12 ozpenne rigate or mezze rigatoni
  • 8 clovesgarlic, finely chopped
  • ½ tspred pepper flakes (pantry)
  • 2 pintsgrape tomatoes
  • 2 Tbspwhite balsamic vinegar (or regular)
  • ½ cupfresh basil, roughly chopped
  • 2 ozricotta salata, shredded (1 cup) — or pasteurized feta
  • —kosher salt (pantry)

Method

Heat the oven to 475°F, rack in the upper-middle. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment. Toss the eggplant with 1½ tsp salt and 4 Tbsp of the oil. Spread in a single layer and roast until browned and tender, 30–35 min, stirring midway. (This is the unattended stretch — do the rest while it roasts.)
Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the pasta and 2 Tbsp salt; cook until al dente. Reserve about ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
While the eggplant roasts and water heats: in a 12" skillet over medium-high, heat the remaining 2 Tbsp oil. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes; cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the grape tomatoes and 1½ tsp salt; cover and cook, shaking occasionally, until they start to release liquid, about 1 min.
Stir in the vinegar, then crush the tomatoes with the back of a spoon. Cover, reduce to medium, and cook, stirring, until it breaks down into a lightly thickened sauce, 8–9 min.
Add the drained pasta, roasted eggplant, and ¼ cup of the reserved pasta water to the tomatoes. Cook over medium, stirring, until the sauce clings to the pasta, 2–3 min. Add more pasta water if it's dry. Taste and season.
Stir in half the basil. Transfer to a bowl, top with the remaining basil and the shredded ricotta salata. Serve.
Pregnancy Fully vegetarian and pregnancy-safe. Ricotta salata is a firm, aged cheese — fine during pregnancy. If you substitute feta, just check it's pasteurized (most US feta is). No other concerns. A lighter night, which can be welcome if the first trimester is making heavy or meaty meals less appealing.
Make ahead (Tuesday evening, ~5 min) Peel and cube the eggplant (store submerged in lightly salted water to prevent browning, then drain and pat dry before roasting). Chop the garlic. The dish otherwise comes together same-night while the eggplant roasts.
For the 13-mo & 34-mo Soft roasted eggplant and pasta in a mild tomato sauce is easy for both kids. There's ½ tsp red pepper flakes in the whole dish — mild, but for the 13-mo you can pull a portion before adding them. Cut the pasta small for the 13-mo. The shredded cheese on top is a kid win; go light if it's a saltier feta.

Pregnancy food-safety, the working rules

  • Meat: ground beef fully cooked to 160°F, no pink.
  • Fish: salmon to 145°F (opaque, flakes easily); low-mercury, which salmon is.
  • Cheese: Cheddar (hard) and ricotta salata (firm aged) are both fine; if subbing feta, check it's pasteurized.
  • No alcohol in any of these dishes.
  • Wash produce well, especially the herbs and tomatoes.

General guidance, not medical advice — defer to your OB/midwife, especially on fish frequency.

Iron, folate & omega-3 this week

  • Iron: beef + black beans (Mon) is the big one; eggplant and tomatoes add a little (Wed).
  • Folate: black beans (Mon), plus all the fresh herbs and the tomato-forward dishes.
  • Omega-3 (DHA): salmon (Tue).
  • Absorption tip: the red pepper, tomato, and lemon across these meals supply vitamin C, which boosts iron uptake from the beans and beef.

Working with the kids

  • Mon (beef & bean skillet): scoop a portion before the red pepper flakes for the 13-mo; mash beans; the cheese is a win.
  • Tue (salmon chraimeh): mild (no jalapeño version); flake salmon and check for pin bones.
  • Wed (pasta alla Norma): soft eggplant + pasta is easy; cut pasta small for the 13-mo.
